## Order of operations (this is what moves the needle)

1. **Union City Council first** (Letters 1 + 8) — the moratorium is expiring *now*; this is the live lever.
2. **Sen. Kemp** (Letter 3) — he's the champion ask; a meeting is worth more than all the rest combined.
3. **Reps. Glaize/Romman/Jackson** (Letter 4) — cheap, sets the 2027 record.
4. **Fulton Commission** (Letter 2) — the abatement freeze is the money lever.
5. **PSC** (Letter 5) — docket comments are public record, do it while Hubbard/Johnson are the new majority.
6. **Governor + Federal** (Letters 6, 7) — lowest per-letter yield, but rounds out the record.

## Follow-up (non-negotiable)

- **Call** the district offices 3–5 days after sending. A call gets logged; a letter alone can sit.
- **Show up.** Union City Council: 3rd Tuesday, 6pm work session / 7pm regular. Fulton Commission: 1st & 3rd Wednesday, 10am.
