# Union City Data Center Fight

Civic-advocacy project opposing the hyperscale data-center buildout in **Union City / south Fulton County, GA** — and pushing for real water, power, and zoning protections.

Maintained by Jalen Lee (VZNS Labs) with Heru. Evidence-grounded: every claim is sourced, and causality is never overstated.

## Why this exists

South Fulton is ground zero for the largest data-center buildout in the country. Georgia Power plans **10,000 MW** of new capacity (~80% for data centers, **$15B+**), and Union City already hosts a **$1.8B Microsoft campus** with a **$75M / 10-yr tax abatement** — with 20+ more projects queued behind it.

This repo is the working record: the case, the strategy, the target contacts, and ready-to-send letters.

## The honest position on the boil-water advisories

Union City went under its **third boil-water advisory of 2026** in October. The direct causes are aging City of Atlanta water infrastructure (a water-main break, plus two pump-station power failures) — **not** data centers, and we do not claim otherwise. The argument is *structural*: a system that fails three times in five months is already at its edge, and stacking industrial-scale water + power demand on top is reckless.
