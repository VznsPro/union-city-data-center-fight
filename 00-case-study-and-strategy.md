## 1. THE SITUATION (source-grounded)

South Fulton County — Union City, Fairburn, East Point, South Fulton, Palmetto — is ground zero for the largest data-center buildout in the country.

**The load reality (verified):**
- Georgia Power plans **10,000 MW** of new capacity — **80% for data centers** — at a cost exceeding **$15 billion** (Spectrum/AP, Dec 2025).
- Prospective large-load pipeline: **16,000 MW (2023) → ~69,000 MW (Dec 2025)**, ~80% data centers (PSC Chairman Jason Shaw, March 2026).
- Consumer advocates estimate overbuilding could raise household bills by **~$3.4 billion, ~$20/month per home** (JD Supra / SACE-Sierra Club briefs, Dec 2025).

**The local reality (verified):**
- Microsoft: **$1.8B** Union City campus, already under construction, with **$75M / 10-yr tax abatement** from the Development Authority of Fulton County.
- Multiple queued projects: Burr "ATL11" (2.1M sq ft), RSC "Stonewall Tell" (1.9M sq ft), TA Realty Red Oak, Vantage GA1, QTS Excalibur (Fayetteville), Atlas "Project Sail" ($17B, Newnan).
- Active rezoning fight at **Buffington Rd / Jonesboro Rd** — Zoning Commission recommended approval; residents mobilizing against it.

**The political reality (verified):**
- Union City passed a **unanimous 5-0, 180-day moratorium on data-center rezonings (March 24, 2026)** — expiring ~Sept 20, 2026. **URGENT.**
- State moratorium bills **HB 1012** (Rep. Ruwa Romman) and **HB 1059** (Rep. Derrick Jackson) **both died in the 2026 session.** Problem was pushed to local level.
- PSC Chairman Jason Shaw published an op-ed literally titled **"Georgia does it right with data centers"** (March 17, 2026).
- Two new Democratic PSC commissioners (Hubbard, Johnson) flipped the Commission's balance — Hubbard is **publicly questioning a 3.2-gigawatt OpenAI contract** (The Current, Aug 2026).

## 2. THE STRATEGY — legal levers, ranked by leverage

1. **LOCAL ZONING (highest leverage, immediate).** Extend the moratorium → permanently reclassify data centers as heavy-industrial/conditional use with setback, noise, water, and screening conditions. This is how communities win (Coweta "Project Oak" DENIED; Denver/Eagan/Ypsilanti moratoria).
2. **PSC (structural).** Force data centers to pay full interconnection/transmission costs. Comment on Dockets **#56002** (2025 IRP), **#56298 / #56310** (2029–31 capacity), **#56008** (large-load rules). Leverage Hubbard + Johnson.
3. **STATE LEGISLATION (2027 refile).** Refile HB 1012 / HB 1059 stronger: statewide moratorium + local control + ratepayer protection + sunset sales-tax break (HB 559).
4. **COUNTY (DRI + Development Authority).** Freeze tax abatements; countywide moratorium; DRI public hearings in affected communities.
5. **FEDERAL (reliability + environmental justice).** FERC/DOE grid & cost review; EPA Title VI EJ complaint on concentration in majority-Black south Fulton.
6. **WATER (EPD + regional water authority).** Cooling-water draw limits; the Fairburn/Palmetto/Union City water authority is already stressed.
7. **COALITION (multiplier).** Join the existing Fulton County opposition group; coordinate with Capital B's Wanda Mosley, Larry Batchlor, and Coweta's "Stop Oak" playbook.

**What "winning" looks like:** Short-term = extend moratorium + permanent zoning. Medium = PSC cost allocation + county abatement freeze. Long = 2027 state law.

## 6. WATER INFRASTRUCTURE FRAGILITY (live, Oct 2026)

Union City went under its **third boil-water advisory of 2026** on Oct 11 (water main break at the Chattahoochee WTP; May + July events were power failures at Hemphill WTP / Adamsville Pump Station). **Direct cause = aging City of Atlanta water infrastructure, NOT data centers** — no official source links them, and we do not claim it.

**The honest campaign argument (structural, not causal):** a water/power system that has failed three times in five months is already at its edge; stacking 20+ hyperscale DCs' water + power demand on top is a reckless risk. This also undercuts the city's own "Understanding Data Centers" page ("no environmental risks found," "no increase in bills").

Full breakdown + fact-check + new council letter: **`02-water-resilience-brief.md`** and **LETTER 8** in `01-letters.md`.
